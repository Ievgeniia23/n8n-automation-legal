Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. About this application

n8n Automation is a private automation application operated by its owner to connect Google services with automated workflows.

2. Information processed

The application may process Google account data required by the permissions granted by the user, including information from Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Google Drive, where applicable.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the automation workflows configured by the application owner, such as reading or updating spreadsheets and managing calendar events.

4. Storage and sharing

Data may be processed and stored on the owner's self-hosted n8n server and transferred to services explicitly connected to the relevant workflows. Data is not sold to third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained according to the configuration of the workflows and connected services. The owner can delete stored workflow data and revoke Google access through their Google Account settings.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect access to the application and its stored information.
